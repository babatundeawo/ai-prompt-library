How it works

From search to clipboard in three steps

No accounts, no configuration — the whole flow happens on the page.

1

Find a prompt

Search by keyword, or browse one of the 11 categories from the sidebar or the library page. Sort by A–Z or by how many fields need filling in.

2

Fill in the fields

Every prompt sits in a live textarea. Replace the [BRACKETED FIELDS] with your own details, right where you're reading — no copy-paste-edit round trip.

3

Copy and go

One click copies your edited version to the clipboard. Made a mess of it? The reset button restores the original text instantly.
