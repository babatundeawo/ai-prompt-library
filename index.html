Why PromptOS

Built to be used, not just browsed

No sign-up, no paywall, no marketplace. A fast, quiet shelf you can open, use, and close.

01

Fill in the blanks

Every [FIELD] in a prompt becomes a labelled input with a live preview. Prefer your own wording? Switch to Edit text and rewrite it freely.

02

Search that keeps up

Every title, category, creator and word of prompt text is searchable, ranked by relevance, from the command palette or the library page.

03

Favourites that stay private

Star what you reuse. Favourites are saved in this browser, stay in sync between tabs, and are never sent anywhere.

04

Works offline

After your first visit the library is stored on your device, so it opens without a connection. Install it from your browser's menu to launch it like an app.
